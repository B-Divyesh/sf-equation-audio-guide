Privacy policy · effective 5 September 2026

Keep article text in your browser

Equation Audio Guide stores your work in this browser. You do not need an account.

What is stored

The app stores your article, script, review checks, and approval states in browser local storage. A refresh keeps your working copy. The service worker saves public app files for offline use.

What leaves your device

This app does not upload your article. The host receives normal web requests when it serves this static site. Those requests can include an IP address, path, time, and browser user agent. This site does not add third-party analytics, fonts, scripts, or tracking pixels.

Delete or export your work

Use Clear in the editor to remove its saved working copy. You can also clear site data in browser settings. Export text or Markdown before clearing when you need a copy.

Questions

Report privacy questions or suspected problems in the public repository.
